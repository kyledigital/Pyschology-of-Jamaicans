Why This Book

Jamaica runs on a second set of rules.

The official rules are easy enough to find. The real ones — the unspoken ones — nobody writes down. They live in how people greet each other, how they move after dark, what they say and what they leave out.

Locals absorb them growing up. The diaspora half-remembers them. Visitors learn them the hard way.

This book puts them on the page — clearly, honestly, and without judgement. Not to expose or embarrass. To equip.

Awareness isn't fear. It's love with open eyes.
— From the Introduction

Chapter 1

Warmth & Welcome

You stop a stranger for directions. He doesn't just point. He walks with you half the way, cracks a joke at the top of the hill, and calls you "my boss" before he peels off. The warmth is real — and so are your boundaries.

The warmth is real. Your limits are wisdom.
Chapter 9

Red Eye & Silent Competition

You buy something new. A close friend makes a joke about it — light tone, quick smile. But the joke has an edge you can't quite name. Later, they go quiet. You try to figure out what you did wrong. The answer is nothing. You just moved.

Not every smile is safe.
Chapter 24

Trust, Contracts & Receipts

You agreed on a price and a timeline. You remember the conversation clearly. When the deadline passes without the work done, you raise it. The response: "That's not what I said." You have no text. No receipt. Just your word against theirs.

If it matters, write it down.
